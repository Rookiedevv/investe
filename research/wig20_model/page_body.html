WIG20 Dip Model

Every evening after the US close the models are asked: will WIG20 fall from tomorrow's open to the close 1, 3 or 5 sessions later? Models are refitted every 126 sessions on past data only, so every probability below was computable on the evening it is dated. Trade entry is the next Warsaw open; costs default to the FW20 round trip.

Out-of-sample scorecard

Can the direction be predicted at all?

AUC measures ranking skill: 0.50 is a coin flip, 0.55 is a weak but real edge, 0.60 would be remarkable for a daily index. Accuracy counts a day as predicted "down" when the probability is above 0.5. The base rate is how often the move actually was negative.

Short simulator

Short FW20 when P(down) exceeds a threshold

Each trade: enter short at the next session's open, cover at the close N sessions later, then wait for the next signal. "Always short" and "always long" use the same calendar with no signal. Cost 0.10% ≈ 38 zł commissions + one tick of spread on one FW20 contract (one tick = 0.024% of the price at 4,200).

What happens after a given situation?

Pick one or more conditions (all must hold on the evening of the decision). The table shows what WIG20 then did from the next open, against all days, over the whole 1995–2026 sample. Consecutive matching days overlap, so a run of down days counts several times; the "day a streak reaches…" conditions avoid that.

Data and method

What this is built on

WIG20 daily OHLCV from Stooq (1991–2026, used from 1995 when sessions became daily), stored in the investe repo and extended every evening by the pipeline. FW20 futures (1998–2026) are stored alongside: they track the index with correlation 0.98 and an average basis of +0.04%, so costs and fills can be modelled on them. Inputs: WIG20 returns over 1–60 days, volatility, range, gap, distance from 20/60/250-day highs and lows, moving averages, RSI, streaks, volume; previous-day and 5-day moves of S&P 500, Nasdaq, DAX, Brent, EUR/PLN, USD/PLN; VIX level and changes; weekday, month, turn of month, expiry week.
